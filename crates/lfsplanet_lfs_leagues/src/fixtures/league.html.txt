<br />
Regulations (up-to-date rules under this link: http://borntorace.eu/fragmaster/seasons/gti-rules.html)<br />
<br />
Our regulations are very simple and let you enjoy what is the most important in our events: racing and meeting your friends! Remember following:<br />
<br />
General Conditions:<br />
<br />
    In order to race you need to have Discord and to join our server – invitation link: https://discord.gg/vKTyGDp (you don&#039;t need to have a mic, only listen).<br />
    No sign-up needed.<br />
    Race takes place at (FM) GTi Thursday host in Live For Speed.<br />
    The server will be password protected before the Qualifications, so come early enough to join us. You can also ask the password on Discord.<br />
    Race clean, respect other drivers.<br />
    Misbehaving might end up with ban.<br />
<br />
Race Conditions:<br />
<br />
    Every Race length is set to achieve approximately 1 hour race (number of laps depends on track). Livestreamed events are in general two shorter races with second race having top 15 of race 1 finishers reversed.<br />
    One mandatory pitstop in each race.<br />
    Qualifications: Race Director calls every driver to do his (or her) 1 hotlap in Qualifying mode (1 outlap, 1 hot lap, spectate).<br />
    Next track and car choice is picked by a winner of previous event The same track environment can’t be picked 2 or more times in a row. Available car picks: separate XFG and XRG, or you can choose both cars at the same time.<br />
    BC (button clutch) is disabled and forbidden. Axls clutch can only be used with H-Shifter. If you are detected your result in the race will be erased.<br />
    Points are calculated in a form to reward top 25 racers with points to overall Championship.<br />
    Crossing yellow line when entering or exiting pits generally is allowed and doesn&#039;t cause any warnings or penalty if executed safely. Any extra rules about pit entry or exit line will be announced few days before start of the race.<br />
    Complaints: You can report everyone who caused crash, misbehaved or disturbed you or somebody in the race - please write a post on the forum or our Discord explaining what happened – every claim will be resolved by admins with further actions taken depending on specific type of claim.<br />
    You can enter our championship with only one name/LFS license: switching names doesn&#039;t affect standings it can end with a ban.<br />
    Cheating and using third party programs to achieve advantage ends up with a ban.</p>
